# Italy Travel Guide

## Overview
Italy offers historical landmarks, architecture, art, regional cuisine and diverse landscapes.

## Rome
Rome is known for its ancient history, archaeological sites, historic buildings and cultural landmarks.

Visitors can explore the city's historical attractions and experience traditional Italian cuisine.

## Florence
Florence is associated with Renaissance art, architecture, museums and historic city streets.

It is particularly suitable for travellers interested in art and cultural history.

## Venice
Venice is known for its canals, historic architecture and distinctive urban layout.

Visitors should plan their movement around the city's pedestrian streets and water transport.

## Travel Planning
- Plan city visits according to the time available.
- Research intercity transport before departure.
- Allow time for museums and historical attractions.
- Explore regional food and cultural experiences.
- Check seasonal conditions before booking.

## Important Considerations
Transport schedules, admission fees, accommodation prices and opening hours can change. Verify current information before travelling.
