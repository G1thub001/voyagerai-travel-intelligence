# Kenya Travel Guide

## Overview
Kenya offers wildlife experiences, coastal destinations, highland landscapes and diverse cultural attractions.

## Nairobi
Nairobi is Kenya's capital and an important starting point for travellers exploring the country.

Visitors can explore urban attractions, restaurants, museums and nearby wildlife experiences.

## Maasai Mara
The Maasai Mara is widely recognised for its wildlife and safari experiences.

Visitors may encounter a variety of wildlife depending on the season and location.

## Mombasa
Mombasa is a coastal destination known for its beaches, Swahili cultural heritage and historical sites.

## Kisumu
Kisumu is located on the shores of Lake Victoria and offers access to lakeside experiences and western Kenya's regional attractions.

## Travel Planning
- Plan safari activities with reputable tour operators.
- Consider road distances when planning multi-destination trips.
- Research accommodation and transport options in advance.
- Respect wildlife and follow park regulations.
- Consider seasonal rainfall when planning outdoor activities.

## Important Considerations
Park fees, transport costs, accommodation prices and entry requirements can change. Verify current information before travelling.
