# Japan Travel Guide

## Overview
Japan offers a combination of traditional culture, modern cities, historic sites and natural attractions.

## Tokyo
Tokyo is a major destination for visitors interested in technology, shopping, food, entertainment and contemporary Japanese culture.

Visitors can explore different neighbourhoods, experience local food and use the city's extensive public transport network.

## Kyoto
Kyoto is known for its historic temples, traditional architecture, gardens and cultural heritage.

Visitors interested in Japanese history and traditional experiences may find Kyoto particularly rewarding.

## Osaka
Osaka is known for its food culture, entertainment and lively urban atmosphere.

## Travel Planning
- Allow enough time to explore different cities rather than rushing between destinations.
- Research transport connections before travelling.
- Respect local customs and etiquette.
- Check current entry requirements before departure.
- Consider seasonal weather when selecting travel dates.

## Important Considerations
Visa requirements, transport fares, attraction admission fees and opening hours can change. Verify these details using official sources before travelling.
